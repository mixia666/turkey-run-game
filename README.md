Turkey Run (Gobble Dash): a turkey sprints from the farmyard through the barn to the kitchen, jumping fences, hay bales, pumpkins and the oven, sliding under the farmer's hanging carving knives and outrunning his dog — grab corn on the way and reach the forest 1200 m away before the farmer catches you.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 and play with Space / click to jump (twice in the air), ↓ to slide, P to pause, M to mute, R to restart.
